TCCS CONTROLLER MANAGEMENT
Create controllers, assign sections and assign SIP operating accounts

Each controller can be linked to one section and one SIP account. The assigned SIP account is used by the browser controller console for SIP registration.

SIP ACCOUNT MANAGEMENT
SIP credentials are stored for the controller console to register the assigned account. The SIP extension/username must also exist in Asterisk/PJSIP.
